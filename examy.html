Exam Pattern
  • This is a Computer Based Test (CBT) for MHT-CET 2026 PCM stream.
  • Total 150 questions carrying 200 marks — each correct answer awards +2 marks. No negative marking.
  • Section 1 — Physics (Q1–50) & Chemistry (Q51–100) — lasts the first 90 minutes.
  • Section 2 — Mathematics (Q101–150) — unlocks automatically after 90 minutes. You cannot return to Section 1 once it locks.
Navigating Questions
  • Click Save & Next to save your answer and advance to the next question.
  • Click Previous to return to the previous question within the active section.
  • Click Clear Response to deselect your current answer for a question.
  • Use the Question Palette on the right to jump directly to any question in the current section.
  • Use the Subject Dropdown above the palette to switch between Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
