📋 General Instructions — MHT-CET 2025

📘 Exam Pattern

  • Section 1 — Physics & Chemistry: 100 Questions × 1 Mark = 100 Marks | Duration: 90 Minutes
  • Section 2 — Mathematics: 50 Questions × 2 Marks = 100 Marks | Duration: 90 Minutes
  • Total: 150 Questions | 200 Marks | 180 Minutes
  • No Negative Marking — unattempted questions carry 0 marks.
  • Section 1 locks after 90 minutes. You cannot return to it.
  • Section 2 is unlocked only after Section 1 ends.

🖱️ Answering Questions

  • Click on an option to select it. Click again to deselect (or use Clear Response).
  • Save & Next — Saves your answer and moves to next question.
  • Mark for Review & Next — Marks the question for review and moves ahead.
  • Clear Response — Clears the selected option for the current question.
